### Grid reorder (4-column, plus sign fix)
- **Grid**: Replaced 3-column flex with `grid grid-cols-1 sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-4 gap-6`. Reordered cards manually: Row 1 = Food & Recipes, Routines, Sensory, Hacks | Row 2 = Communication, Reviews, School, Wall. 8 items × 4 cols = 2 even rows, no orphan. Verified live.
- **Plus sign**: Root cause — `text-gradient` class uses `background-clip: text` with `-webkit-text-fill-color: transparent`. `<sup>` inherits transparent fill and falls outside painted gradient area. Fix: removed `<sup>`, used baseline `<span>` with `text-[1.2em] font-bold text-[oklch(0.72_0.13_360)]` (solid color matching gradient-start pink `#E6A8B5`), and set `-webkit-text-fill-color: currentColor` to override inherited transparent. Verified live — plus reads clearly.
